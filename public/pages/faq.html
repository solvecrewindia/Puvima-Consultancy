PUVIMA Consultancy specializes in three core divisions:
  • Robotics & Autonomous Systems: Autonomous mobile robots, marine waste collection, object detection, navigation & docking, and sensor integration.
  • FinTech & Stock Market Analytics: Market data visualization, historical price & volume analysis, technical indicator dashboards, backtesting, and portfolio tracking.
  • AI/ML & Research Solutions: Machine learning applications, predictive analytics, computer vision, technical project development, and scientific documentation.
When you submit a consultation request through our booking portal, the request is validated on our server and saved to our secure MySQL database with the status pending_review. The system generates a collision-resistant reference ID (e.g. PV-REQ-2026-ABC123). An advisory coordinator reviews scheduling availability before reaching out via email with formal meeting coordinates.
