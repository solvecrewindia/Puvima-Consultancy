Robotics & Autonomous Systems.

"We develop intelligent robotic solutions focused on automation, environmental sustainability, autonomous navigation, and real-world engineering challenges."

Division Overview

Intelligent physical automation for complex environments.

Our robotics consultancy division bridges theoretical algorithmic research and hardware realization. We engineer autonomous systems capable of operating in unstructured terrestrial and aquatic environments, integrating sensor arrays, low-power microcontrollers, computer vision, and deterministic safety routines.

Technical Scope

Areas of Focus

Every focus area within the Robotics & Autonomous Systems division is built on rigorous engineering methodologies.

Focus Area 01 MOBILITY & KINEMATICS

1. Autonomous mobile robots

We design and prototype autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) for industrial logistics, surveillance, and hazardous inspection. Our architectures include differential drive and omnidirectional kinematic modeling, wheel odometry validation, and robust fault-tolerant embedded controllers that maintain trajectory precision under varying payload distributions.

Focus Area 02 SUSTAINABILITY & ECO-TECH

2. Marine waste collection and environmental robotics

Dedicated to environmental sustainability, we architect autonomous surface vessels (ASVs) capable of detecting, intercepting, and gathering floating plastic debris and micro-waste in harbors, canals, and coastal zones. Our designs incorporate solar-assisted recharging, water quality telemetry sensors, and autonomous return-to-base collection protocols.

Focus Area 03 EDGE COMPUTER VISION

3. AI-powered object detection and recognition

We integrate lightweight, quantized neural networks directly into robot on-board edge processors (such as NVIDIA Jetson and specialized micro-NPUs) to perform high-frequency visual identification of obstacles, target objects, and environmental landmarks without relying on constant cloud connectivity.

Focus Area 04 LOCALIZATION & RECHARGING

4. Intelligent navigation and docking systems

Developing robust Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) routines utilizing 2D/3D LiDAR, depth cameras, and IMU data. We engineer precision alignment docking mechanisms with optical markers (AprilTags / ArUco) and infrared beacons to automate battery replenishment and continuous mission readiness.

Focus Area 05 HARDWARE & PROTOCOLS

5. Robotics automation and sensor integration

Creating integrated hardware-software pipelines over CAN bus, ROS2 nodes, I2C, SPI, and UART. We unify multi-sensor inputs through Kalman filtering, ensuring accurate state estimation even under noisy vibration, adverse weather, or partial sensor degradation.
