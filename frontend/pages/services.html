DIVISION 01 // AUTONOMY & SENSING

Robotics & Autonomous Systems

Division 01

"We develop intelligent robotic solutions focused on automation, environmental sustainability, autonomous navigation, and real-world engineering challenges."

Official Areas of Focus (5/5)

  • 1. Autonomous mobile robots: Wheeled and tracked mobile robotics platforms with real-time odometry and obstacle avoidance.
  • 2. Marine waste collection and environmental robotics: Water surface cleaning vessels and eco-monitoring aquatic robots.
  • 3. AI-powered object detection and recognition: Embedded neural networks for real-time visual classification on edge hardware.
  • 4. Intelligent navigation and docking systems: Precision indoor/outdoor localization, LiDAR SLAM, and automated recharging docks.
  • 5. Robotics automation and sensor integration: Multi-sensor fusion with IMU, sonar, LiDAR, RTK GPS, and microcontroller buses.

Engineering Blueprint Visual

Our robotics team focuses on solving physical automation problems with real embedded hardware, robust firmware, and low-latency feedback control loops.

DIVISION 02 // QUANTITATIVE ANALYTICS

FinTech & Stock Market Analytics

Division 02

"We develop data-driven financial technology solutions that help users explore market behaviour, analyze historical stock performance, and visualize financial trends."

Official Areas of Focus (5/5)

  • 1. Stock market analysis and visualization: Interactive candlestick, Heikin-Ashi, and line charting interfaces.
  • 2. Historical price and volume analysis: Processing multi-year tick datasets and order book volume distributions.
  • 3. Technical indicator dashboards: Calculating RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, ATR, and moving average crossovers.
  • 4. Market trend analysis and backtesting: Quantitative framework to simulate strategy outcomes against historic data.
  • 5. Financial data analytics and portfolio tracking: Asset allocation metrics, value-at-risk (VaR), and risk-adjusted returns.

Financial Analytics Dashboard Visual

All financial market analytics models are illustrative data pipelines designed for exploratory research and technical analysis. PUVIMA does not offer personalized investment advice.

DIVISION 03 // INTELLIGENCE & DATA SCIENCE

AI/ML & Research Solutions

Division 03

"We explore artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data analytics to develop intelligent applications and support technology-focused research."

Official Areas of Focus (5/5)

  • 1. Machine learning applications: Supervised, unsupervised, and deep learning models for classification, regression, and clustering.
  • 2. Predictive analytics and data visualization: Forecasting temporal trends and rendering complex multivariate datasets clearly.
  • 3. Computer vision and image analysis: Convolutional networks, semantic segmentation, image preprocessing, and feature tracking.
  • 4. Technical project development: Full-stack software engineering, REST API design, and scalable cloud microservice implementations.
  • 5. Research analysis and scientific documentation: Technical whitepapers, reproducible methodology design, and statistical data validation.
