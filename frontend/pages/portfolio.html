Brain Tumor Segmentation Using Multi-Variant U-Net

Category: Neuro-Oncology Deep Learning

Problem Statement: Gliomas and glioblastomas present irregular diffuse margins and heterogeneous contrast-enhanced intensity profiles, making manual volumetric contouring prone to clinical error.
Technical Approach: Implemented and benchmarked state-of-the-art U-Net architectures (Standard U-Net, Attention U-Net, and Residual U-Net) optimized with composite focal loss and Dice loss on 3D MRI scans.
Technologies Used:
U-Net Architecture Attention Gates ResUNet PyTorch / TensorFlow 3D MRI Volumes
Deliverables & Outcome: Comparative architectural performance benchmark and trained segmentation weights delivering high Dice metrics across Whole Tumor, Tumor Core, and Enhancing Tumor regions.
