Division Overview

Data-driven financial engineering and high-frequency visualization.

Financial markets produce massive streams of non-stationary time-series data. Our FinTech practice develops resilient software systems for ingesting tick data, computing technical indicator matrices in parallel, simulating strategy hypotheses through historical backtesting, and rendering high-frame-rate web charts.

Technical Scope

Areas of Focus

Our five specialized areas of financial technology and stock market analytics.

Focus Area 01 DATA VISUALIZATION

1. Stock market analysis and visualization

We engineer custom, GPU-accelerated charting solutions that enable financial analysts and retail developers to explore equity behavior, intraday fluctuations, multi-timeframe aggregations, and market depth without rendering lag.

Focus Area 02 TIME-SERIES PIPELINES

2. Historical price and volume analysis

Designing high-throughput time-series database models (TimescaleDB, ClickHouse) and data extraction pipelines to normalize historical Open-High-Low-Close-Volume (OHLCV) records, corporate actions, and dividend adjustments.

Focus Area 03 INDICATOR ENGINES

3. Technical indicator dashboards

Building modular mathematical libraries for computing standard and proprietary technical indicators: Simple and Exponential Moving Averages (SMA/EMA), Relative Strength Index (RSI), Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD), Bollinger Bands, Average True Range (ATR), and Stochastic Oscillators.

Focus Area 04 QUANTITATIVE SIMULATION

4. Market trend analysis and backtesting

Architecting event-driven backtesting engines that validate algorithmic hypotheses across historical market cycles. We calculate maximum drawdown, win-loss ratios, Sharpe and Sortino ratios, and slippage simulation to prevent overfitting.

Focus Area 05 PORTFOLIO TELEMETRY

5. Financial data analytics and portfolio tracking

Developing comprehensive portfolio monitoring dashboards featuring multi-asset asset allocation breakdowns, real-time unrealized P&L telemetry, covariance matrices, and risk exposure visualizers.
